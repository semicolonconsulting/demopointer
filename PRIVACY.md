# Demo Pointer Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 2026-10-25_

Demo Pointer does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data.

- **Screen Recording.** Demo Pointer uses macOS Screen Recording access only to show a magnified view of the area around your cursor while you hold the Command key. Captured frames stay in memory on your Mac, are never saved to disk, and are never sent anywhere. Capture stops 30 seconds after you last use the lens.
- **Network access.** The version from the Mac App Store makes no network connections. The version from GitHub connects only to GitHub's API (api.github.com) to check for a newer release, about once a day unless you turn off Check for Updates Automatically, and when you choose Check for Updates. That request includes the app's version number and nothing about you. Neither version has analytics, crash reporting, advertising, or tracking.
- **Settings.** Your zoom level, lens size, on/off choice, and update preferences are stored locally in the app's preferences on your Mac.

Questions: open an issue at https://github.com/semicolonconsulting/demopointer/issues.
