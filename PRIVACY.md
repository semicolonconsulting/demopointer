# Demo Pointer Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 2026-10-25_

Demo Pointer does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data.

- **Screen Recording.** Demo Pointer uses macOS Screen Recording access only to show a magnified view of the area around your cursor while you hold the Command key. Captured frames stay in memory on your Mac, are never saved to disk, and are never sent anywhere. Capture stops 30 seconds after you last use the lens.
- **No network access.** The app makes no network connections. It has no analytics, crash reporting, advertising, or tracking.
- **Settings.** Your zoom level, lens size, and on/off choice are stored locally in the app's preferences on your Mac.

Questions: open an issue at https://github.com/semicolonconsulting/demopointer/issues.
